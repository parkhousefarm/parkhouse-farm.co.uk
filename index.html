The Setting

Parkhouse Farm sits in the Chew Valley, just outside Keynsham — green hills on every side, a market town a short walk away, and Bath, Bristol, Cheddar Gorge and the Cotswolds all within easy reach.

Bath +
Just 20 minutes away. World-famous Georgian architecture, Roman Baths, independent dining, and boutique shopping.
Bristol +
Under 25 minutes drive. Harbourside culture, vibrant food scene, theaters, and Clifton Suspension Bridge.
Cotswolds +
An easy day trip north. Rolling hills, honey-colored stone villages, and classic English countryside walks.
Cheddar Gorge & Mendips +
Dramatic limestone cliffs, cave tours, and breathtaking ridge walks across the Mendip Hills AONB.
Keynsham +
Our local town a short walk or drive away — weekly markets, artisanal bakeries, cozy pubs, and train connections.
